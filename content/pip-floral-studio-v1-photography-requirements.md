# Pip Floral Studio: V1 Photography Requirements
Status: Draft for review
This brief defines the photography needed for the initial website. V1 will not include case studies, so the images need to show the range, scale and quality of Anna's work without relying on individual wedding stories.
The aim is a confident, image-rich site using a focused collection rather than a different photograph for every section.
## Minimum launch collection
The practical minimum is approximately 25 to 35 final images:
- 12 to 16 wedding images
- 4 to 6 photographs of larger arrangements or installations
- 3 to 5 Other Services images
- 3 to 5 Funeral Flowers images
- 2 to 3 photographs of Anna
- 1 studio, making or behind-the-scenes image
There may be overlap between these groups. An installation at a wedding, for example, can serve both the wedding and installation requirements.
## What the collection needs to demonstrate
Across the site, the selected images should show:
- Colour, including work that feels recognisably Pip
- A range of palettes rather than one repeated visual style
- Flowers changing the atmosphere of a venue
- Both intimate details and wider views of complete spaces
- Personal flowers, ceremony flowers and reception flowers
- The scale and construction of installations
- Seasonal variation where practical
- Real settings rather than only tightly cropped arrangements
- Anna as the person behind the work
- Work beyond weddings
- Funeral flowers photographed with care and restraint
It is not necessary to represent every service, season or flower type in V1.
## Home
### Hero
One exceptional wedding image that immediately communicates scale, colour and atmosphere.
Preferred characteristics:
- Wide landscape composition
- Flowers visibly shaping a real space
- Enough clear or calm space for overlaid copy if the design requires it
- Strong at both desktop and mobile crops
- Not simply a close-up bouquet
An alternative mobile crop or companion portrait image would be useful if available.
### What flowers can do
Two or three images showing contrast in scale:
- A wide ceremony or reception view
- A significant installation or floral focal point
- A closer detail showing colour, texture and seasonal character
### Designed around you
One expressive image that feels personal and distinctive. This may be a bouquet, table arrangement or unusual design detail.
### How it works
One behind-the-scenes or making image is optional here. It should feel observational rather than posed.
### Meet Anna
One strong portrait of Anna.
Preferred characteristics:
- Relaxed and recognisable
- In the studio, outdoors or working with flowers
- Space around her for flexible cropping
- Avoid a conventional corporate headshot
### Sustainability
One image that naturally supports the copy, such as British-grown flowers, loose seasonal stems, mechanics without floral foam, or flowers being prepared in the studio.
Avoid images that feel staged purely to signal sustainability.
### Other Services
One image showing work outside weddings. A party table, workshop or seasonal installation would work well.
## Weddings
The Weddings page should carry the broadest image range on the site.
### Hero
One high-impact wedding image, distinct from the homepage hero.
It should show either:
- A complete ceremony or reception setting, or
- A floral installation with a clear sense of place and scale
### Setting the scene
Two or three wide images showing flowers in relation to the venue.
Useful subjects include:
- Ceremony setting
- Reception room
- Tablescape
- Entrance, staircase, marquee or architectural feature
### A few possibilities
Four to six images covering as many of the following as practical:
- Bouquet or personal flowers
- Ceremony flowers
- Reception tables
- Statement installation
- Colourful detail
- A quieter or more restrained design
This should feel like a varied editorial gallery, not a set of case-study cards.
### Flowers throughout the wedding
Use images that make the service categories immediately understandable:
- One personal-flowers image
- One ceremony image
- One reception image
- One installation image
These may be selected from the gallery above and reused through cropping if the layout benefits from it.
### Anna
One image of Anna working, installing or carrying flowers. This should complement rather than duplicate the main portrait.
### Process, investment and sustainability
One or two supporting images across these sections are enough. Suitable subjects include preparation, mechanics, buckets of conditioned flowers, loading, venue work or a finished design being checked.
### Testimonials
Use one wedding image alongside each testimonial only when the image genuinely relates to that couple. Otherwise use a shared gallery treatment around the testimonial section rather than implying a false connection.
## The Process
This page needs a coherent visual sequence rather than a large quantity of images.
Aim for five to seven images covering:
1. Early ideas, samples, sketches or moodboard work
2. Anna in conversation or at a venue, if a suitable natural image exists
3. Flowers arriving or being conditioned
4. Studio preparation
5. An installation being built
6. A finished venue before guests arrive
7. Clearance, bunching or flowers ready to be taken home, if this has been photographed well
Not every process stage requires a literal photograph. Avoid weak or overly instructional images merely to complete the sequence.
## About
Aim for three to five images:
- Primary portrait of Anna
- Anna making or arranging flowers
- Anna within the studio or surrounding landscape
- A detail that connects flowers with colour, texture or Anna's background as a maker
- An optional informal image that adds warmth without becoming overly personal
At least one image should show Anna looking towards the camera. At least one should show her working naturally.
## Other Services
Aim for five to eight images across the page. The minimum useful coverage is:
- Private party or dinner flowers
- Corporate event, launch or larger organised event
- Workshop in progress or completed workshop pieces
- Seasonal installation
- Creative or editorial commission
Seasonal installations and creative commissions remain part of the page even though they are not individual options in the enquiry form.
If there is not strong photography for every category, prioritise the best real work and use fewer images. Do not use generic wedding photographs to fill this page.
## Funeral Flowers
Aim for three to five images.
The collection should feel calm, natural and personal. Useful subjects include:
- A full tribute shown clearly
- A softer detail showing seasonal flowers and texture
- A natural or garden-inspired arrangement
- Flowers placed in their intended setting, where appropriate and respectful
- A detail that demonstrates foam-free construction, only if visually suitable
Avoid:
- Photographs containing identifiable mourners without clear permission
- Images that expose private names, cards or messages
- Visually busy gallery treatments
- Promotional captions or sales-led presentation
The page should use fewer, larger images with more space around them than the wedding pages.
## Enquire
The wedding enquiry view may use one quiet supporting wedding image near the introduction. It should not compete with the form.
The Other Enquiries view may use one neutral floral image that works across different occasions. No funeral image should appear unless Funeral flowers has been selected.
When Funeral flowers is selected, the form does not need to introduce an additional image. The dedicated Funeral Flowers page already provides the appropriate visual context.
Do not place photographs within form progress, validation, error or confirmation states.
## Privacy, Cookies and utility pages
No photography is required for:
- Privacy and Cookies
- 404 page
- Form error states
- Form confirmation states
## Orientation and cropping
The final collection should include a useful mixture of:
- Wide landscape images for heroes and full-width sections
- Portrait images for editorial pairings and mobile layouts
- Square or flexible detail images for galleries
Where possible, retain uncropped originals. The build should create the required responsive sizes and crops rather than relying on pre-cropped files alone.
Important subjects should not sit too close to the edge of the frame. Hero candidates need enough room to accommodate different screen proportions.
## Image quality
Supply the largest available original or final high-resolution export.
Preferred:
- JPEG for photography
- Original colour profile retained where possible
- No screenshots or images downloaded from social media
- No text, logos, borders or watermarks baked into photographs
- Avoid repeated exports that have already been heavily compressed
The build process will create optimised web versions. Anna does not need to resize every image manually.
## Permissions and credits
For every photograph, confirm:
- Pip Floral Studio has permission to publish it on the website
- Whether the photographer requires a credit
- The photographer's preferred published name
- Any restrictions on cropping or use
- Whether identifiable clients or guests have approved publication where required
Funeral images require particular care. Remove private messages and identifying details unless their publication has been clearly agreed.
## File handover
Use descriptive filenames where practical. A useful format is:
`service-subject-location-photographer-sequence.jpg`
Examples:
- `wedding-ceremony-venue-photographer-name-01.jpg`
- `wedding-bouquet-colourful-photographer-name-02.jpg`
- `funeral-natural-tribute-photographer-name-01.jpg`
- `about-anna-studio-photographer-name-01.jpg`
Alongside the images, provide a simple list containing:
- Filename
- Wedding, event or project name
- Venue or location
- Photographer credit
- Permission status
- Any usage or cropping restriction
- Any people who should not be shown
## Selection priorities
If time is limited, select in this order:
1. Homepage hero
2. Weddings hero and core gallery
3. Primary portrait of Anna
4. Funeral Flowers collection
5. Other Services coverage
6. Process and behind-the-scenes images
7. Secondary portraits and optional details
## V1 approval checklist
Before the build is populated, confirm:
- Homepage hero selected
- Weddings hero selected
- At least one strong wide ceremony image
- At least one strong wide reception image
- At least one installation shown at full scale
- Personal flowers represented
- Other Services represented with real non-wedding work
- Funeral Flowers set approved for sensitive use
- Primary and working portraits of Anna selected
- Photographer credits and permissions recorded
- Suitable landscape and portrait crops are possible
- No case-study-specific links or cards remain in the V1 image plan
