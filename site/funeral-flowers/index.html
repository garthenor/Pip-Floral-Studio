Personal funeral flowers, made with care.

I create funeral flowers across South and West Wales, working with you to make a fitting tribute to the person you are remembering.

I consider every enquiry, including those made at short notice, and will reply as quickly as I can.

Enquire about funeral flowers

Flowers that reflect the person.

The starting point might be a favourite flower, a colour they always wore, the scent of lavender or something from their own garden.

You can tell me as much or as little as you wish. I’ll listen to what matters and help you decide how that could be reflected in the flowers.

A tribute does not have to follow a conventional format. Plants, foliage, herbs, fruit or vegetables may also be included to add personal meaning.

Arrangements for the funeral and gathering.

I can create:

  • Coffin sprays
  • Wreaths
  • Sheaves
  • Bouquets and personal tributes
  • Flowers for a church, chapel or ceremony space
  • Flowers for the gathering afterwards
  • Larger or more individual installations

Each arrangement is designed for the person, setting and occasion.

If you are unsure what is needed, I can explain the options and guide you through the decision.

As much or as little help as you need.

Start by telling me the date, location and anything you would like the flowers to express. It is fine if some details are still unknown.

I’ll respond promptly and let you know whether I can help. We can then speak by telephone, video call or email, depending on what feels easiest.

I’ll guide you through the arrangements, prepare an individual quotation and confirm what happens next.

You can be closely involved in choosing the flowers or leave the decisions with me once I understand the person and the occasion.

Everything taken care of.

I’ll coordinate delivery, access and placement directly with the funeral director, church, burial ground or venue.

You will not need to meet me or manage the flowers around the funeral, unless you wish to. I’ll make sure the arrangements are in the right place at the right time and handle the practical details with the relevant people.

Working carefully with natural materials.

All Pip Floral Studio funeral flowers are completely floral-foam-free.

I use locally grown and British flowers as much as possible. The season, availability and needs of the arrangement influence the final selection.

I can also create arrangements for natural burial grounds, taking their individual requirements into account.

Based in Ceredigion.
