What could flowers do for your wedding?

Flowers can change the atmosphere of a space. They can bring colour into a room, frame a ceremony and draw attention to the parts of a venue you love.

From the details of the clothes and setting to the colours and overall feel, flowers can connect the ideas you have gathered and help the whole wedding come together.

A bouquet may be the starting point. From there, flowers can move through the ceremony, reception and wider venue.

It starts with your wedding.

I begin with the atmosphere you want to create, the venue, the season and the parts of the day that matter most to you.

Some couples arrive with a clear vision. Others know the colours or feeling they like but need help developing the rest. Both are perfect starting points.

I’ll show you what could work, explain where flowers will have the most effect and bring the separate ideas into one design.

How it works.

You do not need to know flower names or have every detail decided before you enquire.

  1. Enquire

    Tell me about your plans, date and venue. A likely location is enough if you have not booked one yet.

  2. Discover

    We’ll have a free call to talk through the wedding, the space, your ideas and your budget.

  3. Design

    I’ll create an initial moodboard and indicative quotation, bespoke to you. We’ll review the ideas together and decide what to develop.

  4. Your wedding

    I’ll prepare the flowers, build the arrangements and manage the installation with your venue and other suppliers. On the day, you can enjoy the wedding while I handle the flowers.

How I work with flowers.

I use locally grown and British flowers as much as is practical, taking the season, availability and design into account.

All my work is floral-foam-free. After the wedding, I want the flowers to be enjoyed for as long as possible. I’ll bunch suitable flowers for guests to take away and compost what cannot be reused.
